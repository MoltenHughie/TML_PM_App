# TML (@tml-lifts) — Channel Positioning (draft v1)

## One-liner
**Bodybuilding meets coding:** practical training + real engineering workflows, taught through a systems mindset.

## Who this channel is for (ICP)
Primary (most valuable long-term):
- Intermediate lifters (6–36 months) who want structure and measurable progress
- Tech people (CS/engineering/AI) who like *reasoning, tools, and systems* — not hype

Secondary:
- Fitness-first viewers curious about how an engineer approaches training
- Tech-first viewers who want to get strong/lean without turning it into a full-time hobby

## The promise
When you watch TML you should leave with at least one of:
- A training decision you can execute this week (program, progression, cue, template)
- A workflow you can copy (tracking, automation, tooling, “how I built it”)
- A mental model that transfers between lifting and building (systems-thinking)

## The differentiator (why TML)
- The coding/CS angle isn’t “random B‑roll” — it’s the **lens** (measure → iterate → ship).
- Evidence-first but not academic: *explain the why*, then show the simplest version that works.
- The channel builds artifacts: templates, trackers, scripts, and experiments (viewer gets the assets).

## Content thesis
> **Strong body, sharp mind — built by systems.**

## Tone / vibe
- Direct, pragmatic, no influencer theatrics.
- Respect the viewer’s time.
- Humor is fine; never at the expense of clarity.

## Positioning statements
Short:
> I’m Tim. I lift and I build software. Here’s training that works, engineering workflows that ship, and the systems mindset behind both.

Even shorter (channel description):
> Bodybuilding + coding. Training, tools, and systems.

## What we publish (pillars)
1) **Training Blocks** — programs, progressions, and what changed my results
2) **Technique + Execution** — lifts explained like debugging (cues, errors, fixes)
3) **Build Logs** — what I’m building (apps, trackers, automation) + why
4) **Engineering My Training** — quantified training experiments + the tooling behind them
5) **Deep Dives** — topics that connect training + computation (fatigue models, optimization, etc.)

## What we do NOT do (guardrails)
- Fitness drama/news/commentary
- Hot takes for the algorithm
- “Life advice” without a concrete workflow

## Series naming (recommendation)
Keep series names boring, obvious, and consistent:
- **Training Block:** “12 Weeks to X” / “Block Review”
- **Build Log:** “Building <thing> (Week N)”
- **Engineering My Training:** “Experiment: <hypothesis>”

(We can add a short prefix later, but consistency beats cleverness early.)

## Bio variants
**YouTube About (long):**
Tim — bodybuilder + computer scientist. I build systems for training and for code. Expect training blocks, technique breakdowns, dev logs, quantified experiments, and workflows that connect the gym to engineering.

**Short bio:**
Bodybuilding × coding. Systems, discipline, and progress.

## CTA (what to ask viewers to do)
- Subscribe if you want **repeatable training decisions** and **buildable workflows**.
- Comment with (1) your current goal and (2) your biggest constraint; I’ll prioritize videos that unblock those.

## Quick “does this video belong?” checklist
A video belongs on TML if it:
- Teaches something executable OR
- Shows a real build + tradeoffs OR
- Demonstrates a system/experiment that produces a measurable outcome
