## 3) My Approach to Tracking Workouts (Video #6)
**Series:** [EMT]

**Working title:** How I track every workout (the minimum data that actually moves results)

**Audience:** lifters who hate overcomplicated tracking; tech viewers who like simple systems

**Promise:** A minimal tracking system: what to log, what to ignore, and how I review it to adjust next week.

**Hook (0:00–0:15):** “Tracking isn’t about collecting data—it’s about making better decisions next session.”

**Outline (5–8 beats):**
1. The problem: why most tracking fails (too much, no decision loop)
2. The minimum fields I track (exercise, sets×reps×load, RIR, notes)
3. What I *don’t* track (and why)
4. The review loop (weekly): what I look for (stalls, fatigue signals, progression)
5. Decision rules (examples): add set / swap exercise / deload / keep steady
6. Demo: a real log snippet walkthrough (anonymize if needed)
7. Tooling: spreadsheet vs app vs BB Tracker (show UI briefly)
8. “Failure budget”: how I handle missed sessions without scrapping the plan

**B-roll / visuals:** BB Tracker UI, simple diagram (log → review → rule → next week), gym clips matching the logged sets.

**CTA:** “Want my exact fields + weekly review checklist? Comment ‘TRACK’.”
