## Script v3 (teleprompter-friendly)

### [0:00 – 0:20] HOOK
What's up — I'm Tim.

I lift heavy things and I build software. This channel is where those two worlds meet.

If you're into training, tech, and building a better version of yourself through **consistency and structure** — you're in the right place.

*(quick logo/intro card — 2–3 sec max)*

---

### [0:20 – 1:10] WHO I AM (tight)
Quick background.

Right now I'm doing a PhD in data science. I started in physics, switched to computer science after graduating, and I’m still deep in the "build things / test things" mindset.

And outside of that — bodybuilding. Not just training, but the whole system: nutrition, recovery, planning, and the discipline that makes it work.

I’ve been lifting since I was about 15. A few breaks, but I always came back — and the last few years I’ve taken it seriously as a long-term pursuit.

---

### [1:10 – 2:15] WHY THIS CHANNEL (the bridge)
At first glance, science and iron look like two different worlds.

But for me they’re the same game:
- Show up consistently.
- Follow a plan.
- Measure what matters.
- Adjust when reality hits.
- Keep stacking progress.

That’s the bridge — training that works, and building systems that actually ship.

---

### [2:15 – 4:30] WHAT YOU’LL SEE HERE (3 lanes)
So here’s what to expect on this channel.

**1) Training, honestly**
- What I’m running, what I’m changing, and why.
- Technique and execution — the lifts I care about.
- The boring-but-important stuff: recovery, periodization, and tracking.

**2) Building things (software + workflows)**
- Dev logs and tools I’m working on.
- Workflows that help me stay organized and actually finish projects.
- AI and automation — but only when it’s genuinely useful.

**3) The overlap: engineering your training**
I’m an engineer-brained lifter, so you’ll see experiments with:
- decision frameworks
- routines and constraints
- data and feedback loops

Occasionally I’ll throw in other creative side quests — music production, maybe some game dev — but the core is **training + building**.

---

### [4:30 – 5:15] CALL TO ACTION
If that sounds like your thing, subscribe.

Next up: I’ll break down my current training setup — what I’m doing right now, what I’m tracking, and what I’ll change when progress stalls.

Thanks for watching. See you in the next one.
