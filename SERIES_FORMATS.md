# TML — Content Pillars & Series Formats

## Content Pillars (recap)

### 1) Bodybuilding / Training
Core focus: practical training content for intermediate+ lifters who appreciate structure and reasoning.

Topics:
- Training blocks & programming
- Exercise technique & cues
- Progression systems & deload strategy
- Physique updates & check-ins
- Nutrition frameworks (not meal plans, but systems)

### 2) Tech / Programming
Core focus: real projects, tools, and workflows — not beginner tutorials, but “here’s what I built and why.”

Topics:
- Dev logs (projects, experiments)
- Tools & productivity systems
- AI/automation workflows
- PhD-adjacent technical content (when accessible)

### 3) The Overlap (Hybrid)
Core focus: systems-thinking applied to training — the differentiator.

Topics:
- Tracking & data (training logs, body comp, etc.)
- Automation experiments
- Decision-making frameworks
- “Engineering your training” meta-content

## Recurring Series Concepts

### 🏋️ Training Block (8–12 week arc)
Format: series documenting a full training block from start to finish.

Episode structure:
1. Block Intro (1 video): goals, constraints, program overview, starting numbers
2. Weekly/Bi-weekly Updates (3–5 videos): what changed, why, what I learned
3. Block Wrap-up (1 video): results + what I’d repeat/change next time

Template (each episode):
- 0:00 Hook: “I changed X and it fixed Y” / “Here’s what surprised me this week”
- 0:20 Context (10 sec): where we are in the block
- 0:30 3 bullets: (1) what happened, (2) what changed, (3) lesson
- End CTA: “If you want the full block template / tracking sheet, comment ‘BLOCK’”

Why it works: built-in continuity, natural narrative, easy to batch.

---

### 💻 Build Log
Format: multi-part series on a single project from concept to shipped.

Episode structure:
- Part 1: the problem + constraints + rough plan
- Part 2–N: implementation highlights, challenges, pivots
- Final: results, lessons, what I’d do differently

Template (each episode):
- Hook: show the “before/after” outcome in 5 seconds (UI / workflow win)
- 3 segments max (keep it tight): decision → implementation → what I’d change
- End CTA: “Want the repo / template? I’ll share it when it’s usable.”

Example projects:
- BB Tracker app
- AI workflow experiments
- Open-source tools

---

### 🔬 Engineering My Training (EMT)
Format: standalone videos on specific training systems/experiments.

Examples:
- “How I Track Every Workout (and Why)”
- “Auto-adjusting Volume Based on Recovery: My Rules”
- “Building a Nutrition Dashboard (without losing my mind)”

Template:
- Hook: “I used to do X. Here’s the rule-set that fixed it.”
- System diagram (on-screen): inputs → rules → outputs
- Proof: show 1–2 weeks of logs / outcomes

Why it works: hits the hybrid angle directly; differentiates from pure fitness content.

---

### 📊 Deep Dive
Format: longer explainer on a single topic.

Examples:
- “How I Program My Training Blocks”
- “Understanding RIR (and Why It Matters)”
- “My PhD Research Explained (for Normal People)”

Length: 10–15 min (exception to normal 5–10 target)

Keep it watchable:
- Teach 1 thing, not 5.
- Use a running example.
- Cut jargon aggressively; define any terms in-line.

---

### 💬 Q&A / Community
Format: answer questions from comments/community.

Cadence: monthly or as-needed (once there’s audience).

Guideline:
- Group questions into 2–3 themes instead of rapid-fire.
- Use receipts: “Here’s my rule-of-thumb, and here’s when it breaks.”

---

### 🎲 One-offs / Experiments
Format: standalone videos that don’t fit a series.

Examples:
- Gear reviews (if relevant)
- Day-in-the-life
- Collab content
- Challenge videos (sparingly)

---

## Series Naming Conventions

Use a consistent prefix in titles:
- Training Block: [BLOCK]
- Build Log: [BUILD]
- Engineering My Training: [EMT]
- Deep Dive: [DEEP DIVE]
- Q&A: [Q&A]

Example: [BLOCK] Week 3 — The Change That Unstalled My Squat

---

## Content Cadence (target)

Phase 1 (launch): 1 video/week
- Alternate: 1 training, 1 tech/hybrid

Phase 2 (momentum): 1–2 videos/week
- Once workflow is smooth and backlog is healthy

---

## Notes

- Every video should have a clear audience, promise, and takeaway.
- Avoid beginner tutorial content unless it’s genuinely novel — “here’s what I learned building X” > “how to do X for beginners.”
- Default constraint: can this deliver value in 5–10 minutes?
